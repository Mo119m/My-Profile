\section{An example}
A classic identity:
\[
  e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0.
\]
And a definition: a sequence $(x_n)$ converges to $x$ if for every
$\varepsilon > 0$ there exists $N$ such that $|x_n - x| < \varepsilon$ for all
$n \ge N$.
